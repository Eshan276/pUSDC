Introducing Protected USDC

Protected USDC (pUSDC) is USDC wrapped 1:1, with a delay you control and a backup wallet that can cancel large transfers before they land.

Get pUSDC

Crypto still has no undo button

A leaked seed phrase can empty a wallet in a single transaction.

By the time you notice, the money has already left, and no one can send it back.

Someone can force you to hand it over, in person.

A hardware wallet can't help when the attacker is standing next to you. Instant is the problem.

Most safety nets ask you to give your keys to someone else.

Self-custody shouldn't mean choosing between control and a way to recover.

Protection that only gets stronger

Turning protection on takes one transaction. Loosening it takes a week or more, so a thief can't just switch it off.

Protected accounts

  • Spend up to your own daily limit instantly, exactly like USDC.
  • Bigger transfers wait out a hold window you pick, from 1 to 30 days.
  • Your backup wallet can cancel any waiting transfer before it lands.
See the controls

pUSDC

  • Wraps USDC 1:1. Unwrap any time and get the same USDC back.
  • Unprotected accounts behave like a plain ERC-20, so pools and wallets just work.
  • Incoming transfers always arrive instantly, protected or not.
View on Basescan

Four controls. All of them yours.

Spend

Payments under your daily limit settle instantly. Swaps and transfers work as they always have.

Wait

Anything larger is scheduled and waits out your hold window. You always see when it will land.

Cancel

Your backup wallet can stop any scheduled transfer at any point before it goes through.

Rescue

One emergency action moves your whole balance to your backup wallet and locks it there, even under pressure.
